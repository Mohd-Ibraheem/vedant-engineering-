Vedant Engineering Works

Established in the year 2008, we “Vedant Engineering Works”, are one of the esteemed firms engaged in manufacturing and supplying a comprehensive range of Self Lubricated Plug Valve, Industrial Strainer, Gland Cock Valve & many more. Manufactured using quality approved material and employing advanced methodologies, our range holds a prominent position in the market for its features like high tensile strength, wear resistance, great finish and durability. We are supported by a hi-tech manufacturing plant which is equipped with technologically advanced facilities that enables us in developing the range as per our clients specific needs and demands. Besides, we have appointed an industrious team of professionals that empowers us in developing a qualitative assembly of products in conformation with the international quality standards. Our well equipped R & D unit helps us initiates innovation by constantly endeavoring in finding out ways to controlling manufacturing cost and improvise on our product line. Further, to provide our clients with flawless & excellent quality products, our TQM team undertake quality testing on grounds of certain parameters. Owing to our ethical business practices and client oriented approach and extensive domain expertise, we have managed to maintain goodwill among our esteemed clients. Our entire business operations are efficiently managed by our mentor “Mr. Mehul Shukla”, who has always been the driving force behind the growth and success of our firm. Owing to his wise management & leadership skills and transparent business policies, we have acquired a widespread and loyal client base.
